Find a polyclinic near you

To get medical care in Barbados, find the closest of the nine polyclinics and check its specific services and hours online before you go. While full schedules vary by location, Sir Winston Scott Polyclinic is open 24/7. It is highly recommended to call ahead to confirm availability.
